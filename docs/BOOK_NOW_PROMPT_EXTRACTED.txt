MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — LOVECRY COUNSELLING BOOKING, CONSENT AND GOOGLE WORKSPACE SYSTEM
You are a senior full-stack software architect, security engineer and product developer.
Build a complete, production-ready Book Now and counselling appointment management system for LoveCry.
Website:
https://www.lovecry.ca
The entire client-facing experience must remain inside the LoveCry website. Clients must not be redirected to Google Calendar, Google Forms, Google Drive, Calendly or another external booking website.
The system must integrate with LoveCry’s Google Workspace for Nonprofits account and use:
Google Calendar for counsellor appointment scheduling.
Google Drive for storing finalized signed consent forms.
Gmail or a transactional email provider for confirmations and reminders.
Google Meet optionally for virtual counselling sessions.
LoveCry’s existing admin portal for availability and appointment management.
The organization currently has one counsellor, but the architecture must support adding multiple counsellors later.
